Delete your account and data

In the app

Open Shippy → Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication record) and clears all order/shipment data stored on your device.

By email

If you no longer have the app installed, or the in-app option isn't available on your version, email igal.bogu@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and request deletion. We will delete your account and associated data within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email, name, profile photo) from Firebase Authentication.
  • Any diagnostic identifiers tied to your account in Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics.

What is not affected

  • Your AliExpress account itself — Shippy only reads your order session on-device and never modifies or deletes anything on AliExpress.
  • Order/shipment data already stored locally on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or use "Clear all data" in Settings, independent of account deletion.
Shippy is an independent project, not affiliated with AliExpress or Alibaba Group.
