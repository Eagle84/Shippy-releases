Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Shippy ("the app") is an Android app that helps you track your AliExpress, Amazon and eBay orders and shipments. This policy explains what data the app accesses, what it stores, and what it shares.

Information we collect

  • Account sign-in. Shippy uses Firebase Authentication to identify your Shippy account, via Google Sign-In or an email/password you create. We receive your name, email address, and profile photo (if using Google Sign-In) to display inside the app.
  • Store order and cart data. When you log in to AliExpress, Amazon or eBay inside Shippy, the app reads your order list, cart, product details, and tracking timeline from your own account session, the same way the store's app or website would show them to you. Shippy does not access accounts other than the ones you sign in with.
  • Searches for better deals and price history. To find the same item in the same store, and to show its market price history, the app searches the store's website and a public price-history website (Pricearchive) from your device, using the item's title or identifier. These requests go from your phone to those websites; Shippy has no server in between.
  • On-device AI model. Once, the app downloads a language model file (Qwen2.5, Apache-2.0 licence, about 1.6 GB) from the public Shippy releases page on GitHub, over HTTPS and by default only on Wi-Fi. The model runs entirely on your phone to help check whether two listings are the same product. Item titles are never sent to any AI service. GitHub receives the normal technical details of a download request, such as your IP address.
  • Crash and usage diagnostics. Shippy uses Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to detect crashes and understand feature usage (e.g. how often sync runs, which screens are used). These are standard, aggregated diagnostics — not the contents of your orders.

What Shippy does NOT do

  • Shippy does not read your Gmail or any email account. It never has access to your email inbox.
  • Shippy has no backend server of its own. Your order and shipment data is stored locally on your device (a local Room database) and is never uploaded to a Shippy-controlled server.
  • Shippy does not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Third parties involved

  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication, Crashlytics, and Analytics. See Firebase's privacy documentation.
  • Google Play Integrity and Firebase App Check — used to check that requests to Firebase come from the genuine Shippy app.
  • AliExpress, Amazon and eBay — order, cart and tracking data is read directly from the session you log into on your own device. Shippy does not receive your store passwords; the login happens in a standard secure web view controlled by the store.
  • Pricearchive — a public website the app reads from your device to show market price history for an item.
  • GitHub — hosts the one-time download of the on-device model file.

Some links in Shippy are affiliate links. If you buy through one, Shippy may earn a commission at no extra cost to you; the app says so where such links appear.

Data retention & deletion

Your order data lives on your device until you delete the app, clear its storage, or use the in-app "Clear all data" option. To delete your Shippy account (Firebase Authentication record) and any associated Crashlytics/Analytics identifiers, email us at the address below and we will remove them.

Children's privacy

Shippy is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
