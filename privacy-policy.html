Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Shippy ("the app") helps you track your online orders and shipments. It is available on Android, on iPhone and in a web browser. This policy explains what data Shippy accesses, where it is stored, and what it shares. Where the answer differs by platform, it says so.

Information we collect

  • Account sign-in. Shippy uses Firebase Authentication to identify your Shippy account. You can sign in with Google, with Apple (iPhone) or with an email and password you create. We receive your email address and, with Google or Apple, the name you share, to display inside the app. If you use Sign in with Apple's private relay, we only see the relay address.
  • Store order and cart data. When you sign in to a store such as AliExpress, Amazon or eBay inside Shippy, the app reads your order list, product details and tracking timeline from your own account session, the same way the store's app or website would show them to you. Shippy does not access accounts other than the ones you sign in with.
  • Packages you add. The packages you add by hand or that Shippy reads from your stores: the product name, picture, price, store, tracking number, carrier and tracking events.
  • Settings. Your language, currency, the stores you shop at and your notification choice. If you turn on notifications, a device token is saved with them so Shippy can notify you.
  • Searches for better deals and price history. To find the same item in the same store, and to show its market price history, the app searches the store's website and a public price-history website (Pricearchive) from your device, using the item's title or identifier. These requests go from your phone to those websites.
  • Crash and usage diagnostics. Shippy uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect crashes and errors, and on Android also Firebase Analytics to understand feature usage (for example how often sync runs). A crash report contains technical details such as the device model, the system version and what the app was doing, together with your account's identifier. It does not contain the contents of your orders.

Where your data is stored

  • iPhone and web: your packages and settings are stored in your own account in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), under an identifier that only you can read and change. They are protected by access rules so that no other user can read them, and they are used only to show your packages to you and to send the notifications you ask for.
  • Android: your order and shipment data is stored on your device in a local database.
  • Store sessions: signing in to a store happens in a standard web view controlled by the store. The sign-in session stays on your device. Shippy does not receive your store passwords. Disconnecting a store or signing out of Shippy removes the session from the device.

What Shippy does NOT do

  • Shippy does not read your Gmail or any email account. It never has access to your email inbox.
  • Shippy does not sell your data, does not share it with advertisers and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Shippy does not show advertising.

Third parties involved

  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Crashlytics, and on Android also Analytics. See Firebase's privacy documentation.
  • Google and Apple sign-in — if you choose to sign in with them. They tell Shippy who you are; they receive nothing about your orders.
  • Google Play Integrity and Firebase App Check — on Android, used to check that requests to Firebase come from the genuine Shippy app.
  • Stores such as AliExpress, Amazon and eBay — order, cart and tracking data is read directly from the session you sign in to on your own device.
  • Pricearchive — a public website the app reads from your device to show market price history for an item.

Some links in Shippy are affiliate links. If you buy through one, Shippy may earn a commission at no extra cost to you; the app says so where such links appear.

Data retention & deletion

iPhone and web: you can delete your account and all your data yourself at any time: open Settings and choose Delete account. This removes your packages, your settings and your sign-in account. If you only want to clear the data, use Delete my data in Settings. You can also write to us and we will do it for you.

Android: your order data lives on your device until you delete the app, clear its storage, or use the in-app "Clear all data" option. To delete your Shippy account (Firebase Authentication record) and any associated Crashlytics/Analytics identifiers, email us at the address below and we will remove them.

Crash reports are kept by Firebase for a limited time under its standard retention settings.

Children's privacy

Shippy is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
