Common questions

Why does Shippy ask me to log in to AliExpress?
Shippy reads your order and tracking history directly from your own AliExpress account session so your shipment cards stay accurate and up to date.

Does Shippy read my email?
No. Shippy does not request or use Gmail/email access.

How do I delete my data?
Use "Clear all data" in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored locally. To delete your account record, email us at the address above.

Shippy is an independent project, not affiliated with AliExpress or Alibaba Group.
