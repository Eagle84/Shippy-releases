Why Shippy

Everything you ordered, nothing you didn't ask for

Shippy does one thing well: it keeps your parcels organized and your data yours.

📦

One clean timeline

Every order becomes a single timeline — carrier updates, status changes, and delivery estimates in one scroll.

🔒

Private by design

On Android, your order data is stored only on your phone. On the web, the packages you add are kept in your own Shippy account, visible to nobody else, and you can delete them any time.

🛒

Reads your own orders

Signs in to your own AliExpress account to read your order and tracking history. Nothing is scraped from anyone else.

🚚

Live delivery status

See what's shipped, in transit, out for delivery, or delivered — with estimates so you know what lands when.

🔔

Stay in the loop

Follow each parcel's progress at a glance without digging through emails or app notifications.

⚡

Light and fast

No ads, no bloat. Just open it and see where your stuff is.

Getting started

Up and running in under a minute

1

Install Shippy

Grab it from Google Play and open the app on your Android phone.

2

Connect your account

On Android, sign in to your own AliExpress account so Shippy can read your order history on-device. On the web, add a tracking number.

3

Track everything

Your orders appear as clean timelines — watch each package make its way to your door.

Ready to see all your packages in one place?

Shippy is free and lightweight. Get it on Google Play or open it in your browser and keep every package in one timeline.
