Track your AliExpress orders and shipments in one clean, private timeline — right on your Android phone.

What Shippy does

  • Signs in to your own AliExpress account to read your order and tracking history — nothing is scraped from anyone else's data.
  • Builds a single timeline per order: carrier updates, status, and delivery estimates.
  • Stores everything locally on your device (Room database). Your order data is never uploaded to a Shippy server, because there isn't one.
  • Sign in with Google or email/password (Firebase Authentication) just to identify your Shippy account across devices.
